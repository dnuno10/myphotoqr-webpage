Support FAQ

How do I print my QR code?

Download your QR code from the album dashboard and place it on table cards, signs, invitations, screens or event programs.

What formats are supported?

The album is designed for photos, videos, audio memories and notes. Exact upload limits can depend on the active album settings.

Can guests download the album?

The album owner controls export and sharing options. The final ZIP export is available from the admin experience.

Can I change my album after sharing the QR?

Yes. You can update the album name, description, theme, cover, settings and visibility from the admin panel.
