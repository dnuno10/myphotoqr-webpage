Pricing FAQ

Is this a subscription?

No. The $19.99 plan is a one-time payment for one event album.

How long does the album stay active?

The album and uploaded files remain active for 1 year from the album creation date.

Are audio and notes included?

Yes. The paid plan can include photos, videos, audio memories and notes when those upload options are enabled for the album.

Can I download everything?

Yes. You can export the album as a ZIP with photos and videos.
